Capri screen artwork provenance
===============================

portrait.gif is the unchanged native 498 x 281, 163-frame public Tenor GIF:
https://tenor.com/view/cyberpunk-capri-cyberpunk-capri-edgerunners-2-edgerunners-2-gif-5774155399214368565

The default portrait and speaking loop use original frames 156 through 162.
These match the winking face, raised black hand, hair spikes, and mouth poses
in the supplied reference clip. Frames 156-158 have the mouth closed; 159-162 have it open.
Frame delays are 0.03, 0.04, 0.03, 0.03, 0.04, 0.03, 0.03 seconds (0.23 total).
Short greeting, playful, and arrival reactions use original frames 128-162,
which move from an upright hand gesture through a brief green glitch streak
and finish in the matching wink pose. Frames before 128 are never displayed.

The matching portrait inside the supplied video is about 256 x 138 pixels;
this clean source is 498 x 281. No cursor, TikTok text, scanline overlay, AI
redraw, sharpening, or upscaling is included in the bundled artwork.
The app scales the native source to its window when displayed.

Native PNG fallback frames extracted with ImageIO, without cropping/rotation:
portrait-idle.png, portrait-blink.png: original frame 156 (closed mouth).
portrait-talk.png, portrait-grin.png, portrait-laugh.png: original frame 160.
The source pose holds a wink; no full blink is invented. The blink/laugh file
names are retained for compatibility, not claims of distinct animations.

The additional unused expression cutins are preserved from:
https://github.com/mochiiwuff/capri-desktop
